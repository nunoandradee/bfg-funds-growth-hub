# BFG Funds Launchpad

Build a modern, trustworthy business-funding website for "BFG Funds" (bfgfunds.com), a U.S. small business funding company. Follow this exact page structure and section order:

BRAND

- Company name: BFG Funds

- Tone: fast, reliable, no-nonsense financial partner for small/medium U.S. businesses

- Color palette: deep navy blue (#0E1F3D) as primary, a confident steel/cobalt blue (#2C5AA0) as secondary, white background, one warm accent color (amber/gold #E0A93A) for CTAs and highlights. Clean sans-serif typography (e.g. Inter or Manrope), bold for headlines.

- Logo: simple wordmark "BFG FUNDS" for now (placeholder), leave space to swap in a real logo later.

HEADER (sticky)

- Logo left

- Nav links: Home / Why BFG Funds / Funding Services (dropdown with 6 items: Business Line of Credit, Working Capital, Term Loan, SBA Loans, Revenue-Based Financing, Equipment Financing) / Industries We Serve / Resources (dropdown: Blog, Careers, Case Studies) / Contact Us

- Phone number displayed top-right, click-to-call

- "Apply Now" button (accent color, pill-shaped) top-right

HERO SECTION

- Big bold headline stating funding range and speed (e.g. "$30K to $2M in 24–48 Hours")

- Short supporting paragraph on helping businesses bridge cash flow gaps and grow without bank delays

- Two CTA buttons: "Free Consultation" (primary) and "See Our Services" (secondary/outline)

- Decorative background shapes/gradient blobs in brand colors

- Small rotating/tag list of funding types as pill badges (Business Line of Credit, Revenue-Based Financing, SBA Loans, Same Day Funding)

CLIENT LOGOS STRIP

- Horizontal row of 6-8 grayscale placeholder logos under "Trusted by growing businesses" label

ABOUT SECTION

- Two-column: headline + 2 short paragraphs about BFG Funds' mission to serve small/medium businesses that traditional banks overlook, tailored underwriting approach

- "Learn More" link/button

FUNDING SERVICES GRID (id="services")

- Section intro paragraph

- 6 cards in a responsive grid, each with an image/icon, title, 1-2 sentence description, and "Learn More" link:

  1. Business Line of Credit

  2. Working Capital

  3. Term Loan

  4. SBA Loans

  5. Revenue-Based Financing

  6. Equipment Financing

WHY CHOOSE US

- Section headline + intro paragraph

- 4-column feature grid with icon + short title + one-line description:

  1. Fast Approvals (24-48 hours)

  2. Flexible Options

  3. All Credit Welcome

  4. Personalized Support

PROCESS SECTION ("Our Streamlined Funding Process")

- Intro paragraph

- 4 numbered steps, each with icon, title, short description:

  1. Apply Online

  2. Quick Funding Review

  3. Same-Day Approval

  4. Fast Fund Transfer

TESTIMONIALS

- Headline "What Our Clients Are Saying" + intro paragraph

- Trustpilot-style rating badge (e.g. "4.9 out of 5 stars") linking out

- 3-card testimonial carousel with star rating, quote, name, business type/location

BLOG/INSIGHTS PREVIEW

- Headline "Latest Insights & Resources" + intro

- 3-card grid: image, category tag, title, "Read More" link

- "Load More" button

CLOSING CTA BAND

- Bold headline "Get Funded. Grow Faster."

- Paragraph on funding range $30K-$2M for growth, payroll, inventory, cash flow

- Prominent phone number as a clickable link

MULTI-STEP LEAD FORM ("Have Questions? We Are All Ears!")

- Intro paragraph inviting business owners to get matched with the right program

- Multi-step/progressive form with these fields:

  - Business operating time (dropdown: I currently don't own a business / Less than 6 months / 6-12 months / 1-2 years / 2-5 years / 5+ years)

  - How Much Do You Need (currency input)

  - Gross Monthly Revenue (dropdown with revenue bands)

  - Stated Credit Score (dropdown with score bands)

  - Industry (long dropdown with common SMB industry categories)

  - First Name, Last Name, Mobile, Legal Business Name, Email Address

  - Terms & conditions checkbox with link

  - Submit button

- Style it
